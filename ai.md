AI declaration: Simon Wood (simon@mungewell.org), 2026-10-05.

Within are my views on AI contributions to this project. 

Open-Source relies on the legal framework of copyright to enforce it's licenses. And for that reason I will not accept submissions from AI-agents or code from Generative-AI, as the former can not make legal assignments of copyright and the later can not prove where it 'sourced' the code/information.

Using this other (random) reference:
- https://www.visidata.org/blog/2026/ai/
- https://web.archive.org/web/20260925032610/https://www.visidata.org/blog/2026/ai/

The 'levels of AI' in submissions are limited to:
- Level 0: Human did not use AI at all
- Level 1: Human asked chatbot for ideas
- Level 2: Human coded with minor assists

Ultimately this is my project, I reserve the right to reject any submissions.
